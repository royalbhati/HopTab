Features

Free core, in depth

Two Switchers

⌘Tab, all apps. HopTab replaces the macOS switcher. Your workspace's apps come first, every other running app follows in recent order. Press P and the highlighted app slides into your workspace; 1–9 drops it into any; N starts a new workspace with it. You never open Settings to build one.

⌥Tab, this workspace. Cycles only the apps you pinned here. Nothing to scan past. Prefer the native ⌘Tab? Leave it off and ⌥Tab alone is HopTab's.

⌘+Tab All apps ⌥+Tab This workspace P Pin N New workspace Space Windows ⌘Q/H/M
Pinned apps first, everything else after — pin with one key

Window Tiling

Global keyboard shortcuts snap any window to halves, thirds, quarters, or fullscreen. Works anytime — no switcher needed.

Press the same direction again to cycle sizes: ½ → ⅓ → ⅔. Two universal cycle keys step through every position. Undo any snap instantly. Sixths, ninths and move-to-edge are there for Rectangle refugees, tucked under “extras”.

Drag-to-Snap: Drag any window to a screen edge or corner to snap it. A translucent preview overlay shows the target zone. Edges snap to halves (or maximize/bottom), corners snap to quarters. Toggle on/off in Settings → Snapping.

⌃⌥+Arrows Halves ⌃⌥+UIJK Quarters ⌃⌥+DFG Thirds ⌃⌥+. / , Cycle all ⌃⌥+Z Undo Drag to edge/corner
The everyday snaps up front, Rectangle extras one click away

Layout Templates

Five built-in layouts plus a custom layout editor. Split zones, use presets, or build your own from scratch — perfect for rotated monitors and non-standard setups.

Works with stubborn apps — Chrome, Zed, Wezterm, Electron. Multi-retry positioning with dual strategies for GPU-rendered windows.

Choose a template, assign apps to zones, apply

Workspaces

A workspace is a named set of apps with its own layout, hotkey, desktop and saved window session. Coding, Design, Research. ⌥` switches between them; release and HopTab launches what's missing, applies the layout, moves to the right Space.

The menu bar lists every workspace with its icons. One click to switch. One click to make a new one from whatever is open right now.

⌥+` Switch workspace Per-workspace hotkeys Space auto-switch

Fully Customizable

Every shortcut is configurable. The switcher key, the workspace key, every snap direction, per-workspace hotkeys — record whatever combo you want. Import a Rectangle setup in one click.

Settings is four panes: Workspaces, Shortcuts, Pro, General. HyperKey compatible.

Built-in shortcut recorder for every action

Window Rules

Set persistent rules like “Chrome always snaps to left half on launch.” Define it once, and every time that app opens or gets focus, it snaps to the right position automatically.

FAQ

Good to know
Is HopTab free?

Yes. The whole core is free and open source (MIT). HopTab Pro is a $5 one-time unlock for the automation layer, with no subscription. Students and anyone who can’t afford it can email for a free Pro key.

How is it different from Magnet or Rectangle?

Those snap windows. HopTab also pins your apps for a focused switcher, switches whole workspaces (profiles) per desktop, and automates which profile is active around your calendar, Focus mode, and displays.

Do I need to configure it like yabai?

No. No config file, no scripting, no disabling SIP. Install it, grant Accessibility, press Option+Tab. You get keyboard-driven snapping, layouts, and profiles without the setup.

Does it work on Apple Silicon and Intel?

Yes. It’s a universal binary and runs on macOS 14 or later.

Is it a subscription?

Never. Pro is $5 once, for life. The free tier stays free forever.

Does it collect my data?

No telemetry, no tracking, no account. It’s open source, so you can read exactly what it does.

Why does it need Accessibility permission?

macOS only lets an app move and resize other apps’ windows, and capture global shortcuts, through the Accessibility API. Every window manager needs it.
